Who We Are

About Choice Graphics

At Choice Graphics, we provide high-quality custom printing and branding for businesses, schools, events, and individuals. Using the latest technology, we turn your ideas into professional printed products that stand out.

How We Started

We started small as a shop called "Choice Stationery", offering basic Xerox services. As we grew, we began building and renting photocopy machines to companies. Seeing the increasing demand for premium printing solutions, we launched Choice Graphics and invested in our first DTF printing machine to deliver high-quality, modern printing services.

World-Class Technology

Our Founder, Anil Nor, envisioned bringing world-class printing technology to our customers. He partnered directly with leading manufacturers in China and invited experienced international engineers to install and optimize our production equipment. Today, our factory is fully equipped with advanced technologies, including:

  • DTF & Sublimation Printing
  • Laser Cutting & Engraving
  • UV Printing
  • Custom Sticker Printing & Cutting

Smooth Operations

Great technology is backed by efficient management. Pravin Nor oversees the complete operations of Choice Graphics, managing daily production, quality control, and workflow. While Pravin ensures smooth execution, Anil Nor continues to drive the company's vision and innovation. Together, they have built a reliable printing partner capable of delivering everything from a single customized product to complete branding solutions for businesses of every size.
